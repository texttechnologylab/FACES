## FACES output

### InterView: Towards a Unified VR-Capable Interview Environment { #interview-paper }

<span class="faces-chip faces-chip--review">Under review</span>

**Patrick Schrottenbacher, Alexander Mehler, Giuseppe Abrami, Lydia Kleine and
Doris Stingl** (2026). Submitted to the
[International Journal of Human-Computer Studies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-human-computer-studies)
(Elsevier).

The system paper: the requirements for a flexible, accessible interview
environment; the architecture; and the evaluation study of 27 VR interviews
reported under [Studies](studies.md). Its central argument is that interview
data should be treated as a structured, networked, multimodal dataset:
questionnaire items, multimodal recordings and contextual signals as nodes,
their temporal and behavioural relations as typed links, rather than as
isolated question–answer pairs with metadata attached.

### ReEmote: Towards Emotion Representation in VR Through Va.Si.Li-Lab { #reemote }

<span class="faces-chip faces-chip--accepted">Accepted</span>

**Patrick Schrottenbacher, Alexander Mehler, Vivienne Bernhardt, Leon Rohe and
Giuseppe Abrami** (2026). *Proceedings of XR Salento 2026*, Springer
International Publishing.

ReEmote extends Va.Si.Li-Lab with schema-based emotion mappings that affect both
avatars and their environments, so that human users and virtual agents share an
emotional expression space. The paper sets out the design choices and evaluates
the graphical validity of the representation: emotions can be validly conveyed
through avatar facial expression, and users identify them quickly as Ekman's
basic emotions. It feeds directly into requirement (B) of the FACES platform.
