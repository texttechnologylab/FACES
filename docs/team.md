# Team

FACES is run jointly by the Text Technology Lab at Goethe University Frankfurt
and Departments 2 and 3 of the Leibniz Institute for Educational Trajectories in
Bamberg.

## Contributors

The platform rests on work by the wider Text Technology Lab and the Va.Si.Li-Lab
team: **Giuseppe Abrami**, **Patrick Schrottenbacher**, **Mevlüt Bagci**,
**Alexander Henlein**, **Christian Spiekermann**, **Ali Zandian Ghahfarokhi**,
**Vivienne Bernhardt**, **Leon Rohe** and **Atef Ezziddin**, among others. Full
authorship for each output is given in [Publications](publications.md).

## Contact

Questions about the platform go to the
[Text Technology Lab](https://www.texttechnologylab.org/); questions about the
survey methodology and the study design go to
[LIfBi](https://www.lifbi.de/en-us/Start/Research/All-Projects/FACES).
Issues and feature requests are best filed on the relevant
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/texttechnologylab).
