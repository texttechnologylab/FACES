    InterView: Towards a Unified VR-Capable Interview Environment¶

    Under review

    Patrick Schrottenbacher, Alexander Mehler, Giuseppe Abrami, Lydia Kleine and Doris Stingl (2026). Submitted to the International Journal of Human-Computer Studies (Elsevier).

    The system paper: the requirements for a flexible, accessible interview environment; the architecture; and the evaluation study of 27 VR interviews reported under Studies. Its central argument is that interview data should be treated as a structured, networked, multimodal dataset: questionnaire items, multimodal recordings and contextual signals as nodes, their temporal and behavioural relations as typed links, rather than as isolated question–answer pairs with metadata attached.

    ReEmote: Towards Emotion Representation in VR Through Va.Si.Li-Lab¶

    Accepted

    Patrick Schrottenbacher, Alexander Mehler, Vivienne Bernhardt, Leon Rohe and Giuseppe Abrami (2026). Proceedings of XR Salento 2026, Springer International Publishing.

    ReEmote extends Va.Si.Li-Lab with schema-based emotion mappings that affect both avatars and their environments, so that human users and virtual agents share an emotional expression space. The paper sets out the design choices and evaluates the graphical validity of the representation: emotions can be validly conveyed through avatar facial expression, and users identify them quickly as Ekman's basic emotions. It feeds directly into requirement (B) of the FACES platform.
